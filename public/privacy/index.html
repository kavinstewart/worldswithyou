Website privacy

This notice explains how Exoself Systems handles information connected with this public website. It does not describe a released Worlds with You app. The app is in development and will have its own privacy information before public release.

When you browse

This is a static information website. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account registration, or a mailing-list form. Fonts and artwork are served with the website.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

When you contact us

If you send us email, we receive the address you use and the information you include. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence. Our email providers process messages to deliver and store them. Please do not send sensitive personal information you do not need to share.

Retention and requests

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have sent us by emailing info@worldswithyou.com. We will respond as applicable to your request and location.

External links

If you follow a link to another website, its own privacy practices apply.

Updates and contact

We may update this notice as the website changes. The date above shows the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact Exoself Systems at info@worldswithyou.com.
