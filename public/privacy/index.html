Worlds with You Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Exoself Systems, LLC ("Exoself," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you use the Worlds with You mobile app, our website at worldswithyou.com, and related services (together, the "Service").

Worlds with You is an interactive story app for adults. You play stories and chat with fictional characters whose replies are generated by artificial intelligence (AI). The characters are AI, not real people.

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

Summary

  • Adults only. The Service is for people 18 and older in the United States. We lock accounts when we learn a user is under 18.
  • Your chats power your story. We send your messages to AI service providers so your characters can reply, remember your conversation, and respond to your choices.
  • We don't use your chats to train AI models, and we don't use your private conversations to create content for other players.
  • We check messages for safety. Automated systems check the messages you send for signs of suicide or self-harm risk, so we can show you crisis resources, and for statements that you are under 18.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.
  • You can delete your account in the app. Permanent deletion begins after a 7-day window in which you can change your mind. A few minimal records survive deletion, as described in What we keep after you delete your account.

Information we collect

Information you give us or create in the Service

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an account identifier from that provider, your email address, and the basic profile information your provider shares, such as your name and profile photo. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive an Apple relay address instead. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Chats and story activity. The messages you send to characters, the replies generated for you, the story choices you make, your story progress, and related settings, such as the stories you save or pass on.
  • Age confirmation. Your confirmation that you are 18 or older, the version of the notice you accepted, and when you accepted it. We do not ask for or store your date of birth.
  • Purchases. Your in-app currency (Gems) balance and history, subscription status, and records of purchases and refunds. The App Store or Google Play processes your payment. We do not receive your payment card number.
  • Reports and support. Content you report, and messages you send to our support team, including your email address.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device and app information. Such as your device type, operating system, app version, time zone, language, and identifiers we assign to your installation, account, and requests.
  • Usage information. Such as the screens and stories you open, the features you use, the choices you make, when and how long you use the Service, and whether you enabled notifications. Our analytics records do not contain the text of your chats.
  • Diagnostic information. Such as error codes, crash data, performance data, and request logs. Our request logs do not contain the text of your messages.
  • Network information. Such as your IP address, which our hosting, sign-in, and content delivery providers receive when your device connects to them.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send notifications to your device.
  • Advertising identifiers. Where your device settings allow it, your device's advertising identifier, which helps us measure how well our ads work.

Information from other sources

  • Sign-in providers. Google and Apple, as described above.
  • App stores and payment services. The App Store, Google Play, and our purchase management provider tell us about your purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.
  • Platform age signals. Where available, Apple or Google may tell us whether you are an adult or a minor, or your age range. We store only the result, where it came from, and when we received it.
  • Advertising and measurement partners. If you install the app after seeing one of our ads, an advertising platform or measurement partner may tell us which ad or campaign led to the install.

Information we infer

To run your stories, the Service keeps track of your in-story relationship with each character, for example how much a character trusts you, and what a character remembers about your conversations. Our safety systems also record whether a message was flagged for crisis risk or as a statement that you are under 18.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide the Service. Create and maintain your account, save and restore your progress, generate character replies, remember your conversations, deliver stories and notifications, and let you switch devices.
  • Process purchases. Credit Gems, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and handle refunds.
  • Keep the Service safe. Detect and respond to messages that suggest risk of suicide or self-harm, enforce our adults-only rule, apply content limits, investigate reports, prevent fraud and abuse, and secure our systems.
  • Support you. Respond to your questions and requests.
  • Improve and develop the Service. Understand how people use the Service, measure how well features and stories perform, test new features, fix bugs, and develop new features, using usage, diagnostic, and aggregated or de-identified information.
  • Communicate with you. Send you notices about the Service, including changes to this Privacy Policy, and, if you enable them, notifications about your stories.
  • Market the Service. Measure how well our advertising works and find new players.
  • Comply with law and protect rights. Meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect the rights, property, and safety of our users, us, and others.

We may also use information for other purposes that we describe to you when we collect it, or with your consent.

How we use your chats

  • We send your messages and recent conversation to AI service providers to generate your characters' replies and to run our safety checks.
  • We use your conversations to shape your own story: what characters remember and how your relationships develop.
  • We don't use your chats to train AI models.
  • We don't use your private conversations or choices to write content for other players.
  • We don't use the content of your chats for advertising.
  • Authorized personnel may review specific conversations when needed to investigate a report or safety flag, respond to a support request you make, fix a problem, or comply with the law. When we review flagged messages, we remove information that identifies you first. We don't add your messages to the data we use to test or tune our safety systems.

AI features

The characters in Worlds with You are fictional, and their replies are generated by AI. AI can make mistakes, and characters may say things that are inaccurate or unexpected. Don't rely on a character for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

To generate replies and run safety checks, we send the content of your messages and relevant story context to third-party AI service providers. We do not attach your account's name or email address to these requests, but a provider receives anything you include in your messages. Each provider processes this information on our behalf under its agreement with us. Some providers may keep it for a limited time under their own terms, for example to monitor for abuse.

We currently use these AI service providers:

  • OpenAI, to generate character replies.
  • Typesafe (typesafe.ai), to run safety checks and to evaluate how your story relationships develop.
  • OpenRouter, which routes a request to a model host, as a backup for our crisis check when our primary safety provider is unavailable.

We may change our AI service providers. When we do, we will update this list.

Please don't share information in chats that you don't want processed this way, such as your address, financial account details, or health details about other people.

Safety features

Crisis detection

California and New York law require companion chatbot operators to detect when a user may be at risk of suicide or self-harm and to refer them to crisis services. Automated systems, run by third-party AI service providers, check each message you send, together with recent conversation, for signs of suicidal thoughts or self-harm. No automated system is perfect: a check can miss a message, and if our safety providers are unavailable, a message may be delivered without a check. If a message is flagged, we show you crisis resources, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, and the character's reply changes. Flagged messages are not used for the character's memory or relationship tracking.

A flag does not by itself cause us to contact anyone. We may disclose information to emergency services or others only as described in How we share information, for example when we believe it is necessary to prevent imminent harm. Beginning in 2027, our yearly report to the State of California contains the number of times we showed crisis resources and a description of our protocols, with no information that identifies any user.

Details of our crisis protocol, including how we prevent, detect, and respond to suicide and self-harm content, are published on our website, worldswithyou.com.

Adults only and the under-18 lock

The Service is for adults. If Apple or Google reports that you are a minor, or a message you send clearly states that you are under 18, we lock your account. A locked account can't play stories, chat, or make purchases. You can still sign out or delete your account.

  • Lock from a platform age signal. The app also remembers the result on that device, so signing out or creating a new account on the device stays locked. The app checks the platform signal again at each launch, and a later adult result from Apple or Google unlocks the account and the device.
  • Lock from a message. Only a support review lifts this lock. If you believe the lock is a mistake, contact us to request a review.

Reports and content limits

You can report character messages and media. We review reports and may keep the reported content and related information to investigate and act on them. Automated checks also review generated replies before you see them.

Sensitive information and health data

Your chats may include sensitive information that you choose to share, such as details about your sex life, sexual orientation, or mental or physical health. Our crisis detection also infers whether a message suggests a risk of suicide or self-harm, which some state laws treat as health data.

We use this information only to provide the Service, including generating replies and running our safety features, to keep the Service safe and secure, and for the legal, safety, and business transfer purposes described in How we share information. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising. Before your first chat message, we ask you to agree to this processing, and we record your agreement with the date and the version of the notice you saw. We do not process your chat messages until you agree. You can withdraw your consent at any time by stopping use of the chat features and deleting your account, as described in Delete your account.

Your choices and account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app under Account > Delete account. You can also request deletion by emailing support@worldswithyou.com. We will verify that you control the account before we delete it.

  • When you ask to delete your account, we sign you out on every device and schedule the account for deletion 7 days later.
  • If you sign in again within those 7 days, your account is restored.
  • After 7 days, we begin permanently deleting your account, chats, story progress, Gems, and subscription status from our live systems, and your account records with our sign-in and purchase management providers. This usually finishes shortly after the 7 days end.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play to stop billing.
  • Copies may remain in backups until they expire. Your app store keeps its own purchase records. Usage information in our analytics systems is kept as described in How long we keep information.

What we keep after you delete your account

We keep a minimal record that the account existed and was deleted: its random account ID, its status, and the dates it was created, deleted, or restored. It contains no name, email, chats, or story data. We keep it so that an old sign-in session can never recreate a deleted account. We also keep security and operational records that hold only random IDs, counts, and times, as described in How long we keep information.

If your account was locked because a message said you were under 18, we keep a one-way, keyed code (a "hash") made from the account identifier your sign-in provider gave us. We cannot turn this code back into your identifier, and we keep nothing else with it except the reason and date. If someone later signs in with the same Google or Apple account, we recognize the code and lock the new account too. This keeps our adults-only rule from being bypassed by deleting and recreating an account. We keep the code until a support review clears it. To request a review, email support@worldswithyou.com.

Other choices

  • Notifications. You can turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • Ad tracking. On iPhone, you can decline or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings. On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Reset a story. Resetting your playthrough starts your stories fresh. It does not delete your account or its records; to do that, delete your account.
  • Sign out. Signing out ends your session on that device. Your account remains.

  • Portability. Receive your personal information in a portable format.
  • Opt out. Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, and profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make such decisions.
  • Sensitive data. Withdraw your consent to our processing of sensitive data, as described in Sensitive information and health data.
  • Appeal. Appeal our decision about your request.

How to make a request. Email support@worldswithyou.com. Tell us what you are requesting and the state you live in. If you can, email us from the address linked to your account. To opt out of sharing or targeted advertising, put "Do Not Share" in the subject line; you can also use the device settings in Other choices. You can delete your account at any time in the app. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal, where the law requires it, as a request to opt out of sale and sharing for the browser that sends it and, if we know which account it belongs to, for that account.

Verification. We verify requests by confirming you control the account, for example by asking you to sign in with it, to email us from its address, or, for guest play, to show that you control the device you used. We may ask for more information if needed, but not more than necessary. We don't need to verify an opt-out request.

Authorized agents. You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We may require proof that the agent is authorized and ask you to verify your identity directly.

Response. We respond within the time the law requires, usually 45 days, and tell you if we need more time.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.

No discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Some requests, such as deleting your data, may mean we can no longer provide parts of the Service.
