Every world opens on film.

Each story starts as a short, vertical, cinematic scene, the kind you already binge before bed. Then it stops and hands you the phone.

You decide what to say. The story plays out your way. And the character you just met is waiting in your inbox.

He remembers the three cucumber coins.

When the scene ends, the conversation keeps going. Characters answer in their own voice, and they remember what happened between you: the choices you made and the details you told them.

Wes knows only what you tell him. He never texts first. You set the pace.

An example of how a chat with Wes can go. In the app, his replies are generated by AI, in his voice, from your story.

Can I play yet?

Not yet. We’re finishing the first worlds now, and there’s no public release date. Ask for launch news and we’ll send you one email when it opens.

Who is it for?

Adults 18 and over who love romance: short dramas, romance novels, otome and choice games. Every romanceable character is an adult.

How does AI fit in?

Each world’s film, choices, and story are authored and reviewed before release. In chat, characters’ replies are generated by AI, in their voice, from what has happened in your story, including the choices you made.

Are the characters real people?

No. Characters and stories are fictional. Their responses may be generated by AI. This experience is for adults.

What happens to my chats?

We send your messages to AI providers so characters can reply and remember. We don’t use your chats to train AI models, and we don’t sell your personal information. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
