What is Worlds with You?

An app of short, cinematic romance stories you step into. Each story opens with a filmed scene, seen through your eyes. Then it carries on as a text conversation with him.

How does it work?

Watch a scene. When it matters, the story asks what you say, and what you choose changes what he says back. After the scene, keep texting him whenever you like. He remembers what happened between you.

Who writes the stories, and where does AI come in?

Films, choices and stories are written and reviewed before release. In chat, his replies are generated by AI in the character’s voice, from your story.

Are the characters real?

No. The characters and their stories are fiction.

What happens to my chats?

We don’t use your chats to train AI models, and we don’t sell your personal information. The privacy policy has the details.

When can I play?

Worlds with You is still in development, and there’s no release date yet. Tell us you’d like to know, and we’ll send one email when it opens.
