# Website media

All media on this site is original to Worlds with You. The founder approved it for use on this website on 2026-09-30. That approval does not extend to the app.

## Not Jess footage and stills

`assets/media/steakhouse-grant-orders.mp4`, `sno-top-loop.mp4` and their posters, `04-grant-eye-contact.webp` and `06-your-hand-sets-down-cash.webp` come from the opening of *Not Jess*, an original Worlds with You story. The footage was generated from text with our own original image references. No real person's likeness was requested, and all signage is invented. The clips were trimmed and re-encoded for the web without changing their content.

## Wes portrait

`assets/media/wes-portrait.webp` is the founder-approved canonical portrait of Wes, an original fictional adult character. It was generated with an explicit instruction against resembling any real person. It was resized and encoded as WebP for delivery.

## Fonts

`assets/fonts/` holds latin subsets of Jost and Newsreader, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-jost.txt`, `OFL-newsreader.txt`).

## Placeholder story covers

The bookshelf's seven covers are typographic placeholders with invented titles. They illustrate the range of stories and are not product listings.
